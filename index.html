HOW TO READ IT
  • Dots are wallets and contracts. Bigger means more volume in the window.
  • Lines are transfers between two addresses. Brighter means more of them.
  • Sparks travel from sender to receiver along the most recent transfers.
  • Green rings mark liquidity pools. Big hubs are routers, pool managers and the mint address.
  • Colour is the coin each address moves most. Toggle coins in the legend, drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover for details.
  • Find a wallet flies to any address. If it is not on the map yet, its last six hours of moves are pulled from the chain and it lands next to its counterparties.
  • Live means it: every few seconds the page reads the newest blocks, and fresh transfers run bright from sender to receiver.
